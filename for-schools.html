How a request runs

From the request to the record, in one place.

01

The request

Items, quantities, the need-by date and the must-haves: a whiteboard size, a delivery window, a DBS requirement. Must-haves disqualify, so a cheaper quote that fails one cannot be awarded by mistake.

02

The quotes

Suppliers reply to an ordinary email. Each reply is read, matched to your lines, flagged where unclear, and locked at the deadline. A late quote is held, not slipped in.

03

The decision

One sheet, cheapest compliant quote marked, negotiation notes per supplier, a recommendation with reasons. The budget holder signs off from an email link; no account needed.

04

The record

The frozen award sheet, every reading, every scoring run, every change and every sign-off, downloadable as one Excel or PDF record. Internal scrutiny and the external auditor get the same file.

Is it for you

A good fit, and not.

A good fit

A trust running from a few to a few dozen requests a month across its schools, with one or two people in central finance responsible for showing competition took place.

Probably not

A trust with a full procurement function and a tendering platform already in use for everything. Bidalize sits below formal tenders, not instead of them.

Doing fewer than a handful of requests a month? A spreadsheet is fine and we will tell you so. Bidalize earns its keep from about ten requests a month, or earlier when quotes come in several languages or someone has to answer for the decision.

Questions

Before you ask

Does this cover the Academy Trust Handbook requirements?

It gives you the evidence the handbook asks for: that quotes were sought, how they were compared, who decided and who approved. What your scheme of delegation sets as thresholds and approvers is yours to decide; Bidalize records that you followed it.

We already have a finance system

Keep it. Bidalize produces the comparison and the decision record; the purchase order and payment stay where they are. A CSV with fixed column names is there if you want to import the award.

Who in the trust uses it?

Usually one or two people in central finance set it up, and school office or site staff raise requests. Everyone who needs to see a request can be given a login; logins are not charged.

Can we try it on real purchases?

Yes. The first 5 requests are free, no card, no time limit. Run your next real request through it and compare the file you get with what you keep today.
