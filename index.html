Sourcing · How it works

From requisition to award, in one place.

Six steps, and every one of them is on the record.

01

Start from the requisition

Upload the PR. The AI drafts the request — items, quantities, budget, who asked for it. The requisition stays attached.

02

Send it to suppliers

One email to each supplier. Must-have requirements are marked pass/fail, so suppliers know up front what disqualifies them.

03

Quotes are read for you

Replies land on the request by themselves. Lock the quotes at the deadline; a late quote is held, not slipped in.

04

Compare and negotiate

One sheet, like Excel, where every figure knows where it came from. Negotiation notes tell you what to ask each supplier.

05

Award — whole or split

Award one supplier, or copy the sheet to give different items to different suppliers. Sign-off goes by email.

06

Keep the record

The awarded sheet is frozen and every step is in the audit trail. Print it, download it in Excel, or export the data for your ERP.

Questions

Before you ask

Do my suppliers need to sign up?

No. They reply to your email with their quote, in whatever form they already use: a PDF, a photo of a printed or handwritten quote, a spreadsheet, or prices typed in the email.

What exactly is a request?

One purchase sent to suppliers for quotes, however many items it has and however many suppliers you send it to. Five items to three suppliers is one request. It counts when you send it; a draft does not count, and a request cancelled before any quote arrives is given back.

What happens when we run out of requests?

You can finish anything already started. To start more, move up a plan, or buy a block of 10 that is valid for 12 months. Nothing stops mid-request and nothing is lost.

Why price by request and not by user?

Because the work, and our cost, is in reading and comparing quotes, not in who logs in. Charging per login would make you think twice before inviting your finance colleague or the approver, and the record is better when they are in it. A company also knows roughly how many requests it sends a month; it rarely knows how many quotes it will receive, so we never count quotes.

What if the AI reads a figure wrong?

Every figure shows where it was read from, unclear ones are flagged for you, and you can type over any of them. The original reading is kept, the change is logged, and the reading itself is in the audit trail with the model used and its confidence.

Can I compare quotes in different currencies?

Yes. Each quote stays in its own currency and is converted to yours using the rates you set, and the rate used is shown on the sheet, in the downloads and in the audit trail of the scoring run.

What does the audit trail record?

Who did what and when on each request: the requisition, sending, locking and reopening quotes, every automatic reading of a quote, every scoring run with the scores it produced, deletions with their reason, every figure typed over with its old and new value, awards, sign-offs and reversals. Entries cannot be edited or removed.

Is Bidalize a replacement for Ariba, Coupa or our ERP?

No. Those run contracts and purchase orders for the spend they cover. Bidalize is for the requests that never reach them. Today it exports a CSV with fixed column names that your ERP or reporting tool can import; a direct connection is not available yet.

What about quotes that arrive on WhatsApp?

Forward them, or save the photo or PDF and upload it to the request. They are read the same way as emailed quotes.

What happens to our data if we stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. If you cancel, the plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. If a payment fails, Paddle tries the card again for up to 30 days and everything keeps working. After that your organisation moves to the free tier: you can still open and download all of it, and finish requests already started. Choosing a plan again restores full use at once. The detail is in the Terms.

Do we sign a contract?

An administrator accepts a one-page Customer Agreement when creating the account. If your company needs a copy signed by both parties, we can provide one.
