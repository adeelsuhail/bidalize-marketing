Sourcing · How it works

From requisition to award, in one place.

Six steps, and every one of them is on the record.

01

Start from the requisition

Upload the PR. The AI drafts the request — items, quantities, budget, who asked for it. The requisition stays attached.

02

Send it to suppliers

One email to each supplier. Must-have requirements are marked pass/fail, so suppliers know up front what disqualifies them.

03

Quotes are read for you

Replies land on the request by themselves. Lock the quotes at the deadline; a late quote is held, not slipped in.

04

Compare and negotiate

One sheet, like Excel, where every figure knows where it came from. Negotiation notes tell you what to ask each supplier.

05

Award — whole or split

Award one supplier, or copy the sheet to give different items to different suppliers. Sign-off goes by email.

06

Keep the record

The awarded sheet is frozen and every step is in the audit trail. Print it, download it in Excel, or export the data for your ERP.

Sourcing · Smart comparison

The spreadsheet you build today — without the retyping.

One column per supplier, one row per item, in your currency. Type over any figure; the original reading is kept. The colours tell you where to look.

Green: cheapestCompared with tax included, and never a disqualified quote.
Amber: checkA figure is missing or unclear, or items were removed and the tax may no longer fit.
Blue: typed by youChanged by hand. The reading from the quote stays on file, and the change is logged.
Red: disqualifiedFails a must-have requirement. It can't be ranked or awarded, and the reason is one click away.
  • Charges in any currencyDuties in local currency, a courier in USD — each charge in its own currency, and anything paid to others stays off the supplier's PO.
  • Tax, delivery and payment terms always on the sheetThey can be zero, but they can't be removed — so a cost can't quietly drop out of the comparison.
  • Several sheets per requestCopy a sheet to try a split award or a different set of suppliers, without losing the first one.
  • AI calculatorDescribe a sum — "18% on the two items left" — check the working, and put the answer in a cell.
Sourcing · Negotiation notes

Know what to ask each supplier before you call them.

For every supplier, Bidalize writes down where they stand: how far they are from the leader, which items they're beaten on and by how much, and which terms are weaker. Every number is a cell on the sheet, so you can quote it on the phone.

Sourcing · Audit trail and record

When someone asks "why this supplier?", the answer is already written down.

Every step has a name and a time on it — from the requisition to the award. Log entries can't be edited or deleted, and the steps that change the outcome need a reason.

The sheet's log in the app: who typed over what, with the value before.

Quotes locked before award

Nothing can arrive and change the comparison afterwards. Reopening needs a reason, and it's logged.

Every change logged

Who typed over which figure, what it was before, and when. Deleting a quote needs a reason, and what was deleted is kept.

Frozen award sheets

The sheet an award was made from can't be edited. Reversing an award needs a reason, and both stay on the record.

Sign-off by email

Approvers get a link with the sheet and the reasons it needs sign-off. No account needed, and they don't count as logins.

Split awards

Different items to different suppliers, each with its own PO amount, and duties paid to others kept separate.

Printable record

Request, quotes, comparison, decision and sign-off on one document, ready to file.

Questions

Before you ask

Do my suppliers need to sign up?

No. They reply to your email with their quote, in whatever form they already use.

What if the AI reads a figure wrong?

Every figure shows where it was read from, unclear ones are flagged for you, and you can type over any of them. The original reading is kept and the change is logged.

Can I compare quotes in different currencies?

Yes. Each quote stays in its own currency and is converted to yours using the rates you set, and the rate used is shown on the sheet and in the downloads.

What does the audit trail record?

Who did what and when on each request: the requisition, sending, locking and reopening quotes, deletions with their reason, every figure typed over with its old and new value, awards, sign-offs and reversals. Entries can't be edited or removed.

How do negotiation notes work?

For each supplier they list the gap to the leader, the items where someone else is cheaper and by how much, and terms that are weaker than the best offer. They're worked out from the sheet, so they change when the sheet changes.

Does it connect to my ERP?

Today it exports a CSV with fixed column names that your ERP or reporting tool can import. A direct connection isn't available yet.

What about quotes that arrive on WhatsApp?

Forward them, or save the photo or PDF and upload it to the request. They're read the same way as emailed quotes.

How is it priced?

You can run 5 sourcing requests free in your first 30 days, with every feature. After that, Team is for up to 5 logins and Business for up to 10, paid monthly with no long contract. Supplier Check is free on every plan while it is in early access, and approvers who sign off by email are always free.

What happens to our data if we stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. If you cancel, the plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. If a payment fails, Paddle tries the card again for up to 30 days and everything keeps working. After that your organisation moves to the Free plan: you can still open and download all of it, and finish requests already started. Choosing a plan again restores full use at once. The detail is in the Terms.

Do we sign a contract?

An administrator accepts a one-page Customer Agreement when creating the account. If your company needs a copy signed by both parties, we can provide one.
