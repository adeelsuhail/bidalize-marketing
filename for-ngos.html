The situation

Where the hours and the audit questions come from.

  • The donor rule is per purchase, the capacity is notEach grant agreement sets its own thresholds for one, three or more quotes. A country office may run a hundred such purchases a quarter with one logistics officer. Bidalize makes each one a request with its own file, so the evidence exists at the time, not reconstructed at audit.
  • Local suppliers quote by handA photographed handwritten quote on letterhead is the norm in many markets. Bidalize reads it, shows where each figure came from, and flags what it is not sure of instead of guessing.
  • Currency and small printQuotes in local currency against a budget in USD or EUR, handling charges in a footnote, a cheaper offer that quietly changes the specification. Bidalize converts at the rate you set, pulls charges into the open and disqualifies quotes that fail a must-have.

How a request runs

From the request to the record, in one place.

01

The request

Items, quantities, specification and must-haves, with the grant or budget line attached. Suppliers see the must-haves up front.

02

The quotes

Email replies, PDFs, photos, or a quote forwarded from WhatsApp. Read, matched, flagged, locked at the deadline. Late quotes are held and logged.

03

The decision

One sheet in your reporting currency, the cheapest compliant quote marked, a recommendation with reasons, and sign-off by the budget holder or country director from an email link.

04

The record

Readings, scoring runs, changes, sign-offs and the frozen award sheet in one downloadable file per purchase, with the exchange rates used. One file per request is what the donor auditor wants.

Is it for you

A good fit, and not.

A good fit

A country office or programme team running a handful to a hundred purchases a month under donor rules, with quotes arriving in local currency and mixed formats.

Probably not

A headquarters procurement function running international tenders through a dedicated system. Bidalize is for the field purchases below that line.

Doing fewer than a handful of requests a month? A spreadsheet is fine and we will tell you so. Bidalize earns its keep from about ten requests a month, or earlier when quotes come in several languages or someone has to answer for the decision.
