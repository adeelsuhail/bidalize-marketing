Is it for you

Who we are looking for.

A good fit

A company, clinic, school, NGO or contractor that regularly asks several suppliers for quotes: spare parts, packaging, IT equipment, services, materials. Someone there compares the quotes by hand today and has to explain the choice afterwards.

Not a fit

Suppliers looking for buyers, students, personal purchases, or anyone who will not run a real request in the 45 days. If that is you, the free 5 requests are still yours, no application needed.

Questions

Before you apply

Is it really free?

Yes. No card is asked for during the tester period, and nothing is charged.

What happens after 45 days?

Any tester requests you have not used expire and the account carries on as a free account. Nothing renews and nothing is billed unless you choose a plan yourself. If you do, within 30 days, the 20% discount applies.

Do my suppliers need an account?

No. They get the request by email and reply the way they always do: an email, a PDF, a photo or a spreadsheet. Approvers sign off from a link, also without an account.

Why only 20 places?

Because each tester gets a real conversation with the founder, and that does not scale. When the 20 places are taken, applications close.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your account and is used to run your requests. Your feedback may be used to improve the product. The details are in the privacy policy.
